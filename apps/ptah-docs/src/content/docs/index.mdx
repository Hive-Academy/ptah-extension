## Start here

New to Ptah? Follow these three pages in order. They take you from download to a working agent roster.

<Steps>

1. **[Install Ptah](/getting-started/installation/)** — download the desktop app for Windows, macOS, or Linux.

2. **[Connect a provider](/getting-started/signing-in/)** — bring your own key for Claude, Copilot, Codex, Ollama, or any other supported provider.

3. **[Run the setup wizard](/setup/setup-wizard/)** — let Ptah analyze your repository and generate an agent roster tuned to your stack.

</Steps>

  <Card title="Chat with a real team" icon="comment-alt">

## Good to know

- **Every local capability is free.** Ptah is open source. Nothing on your own
  machine sits behind a paywall. See the [tier comparison](/reference/tier-comparison/).
- **Your keys stay on your machine.** Ptah never proxies your traffic through a
  service we control. See [Providers](/providers/).
- **These pages describe the desktop app by default.** The same core also ships
  as a [VS Code extension](/vscode-extension/) and a [headless CLI](/cli/).
