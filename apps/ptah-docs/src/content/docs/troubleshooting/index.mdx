Most problems are in one of a small number of categories. Each page in this section uses the form **Symptom → Likely cause → Fix**.

## Collect information first

Before you look at a category, collect the basics for a bug report:

- The Ptah version and your OS.
- The log file of the day. See [Logs & diagnostics](/troubleshooting/logs-and-diagnostics/).
- The steps that cause the problem.
