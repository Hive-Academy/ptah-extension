# Fix C2 scoped code-logic re-review

**Score: 6/10. Verdict: REVISE.** F-A, F-B and F-C are fixed for `anubis` and `anubis-light`. The rules are also active in the 32 selectable built-in themes, where the focus frame and pill text still miss the requested contrast thresholds.

## Numbered findings

1. **Serious — focused frame remains below 3:1 in other shipped themes.** `libs/frontend/chat-ui/src/lib/molecules/compact-session/compact-rail-card.component.ts:55-63,168-171`. The `:host-context` exception only covers `anubis-light`; every other theme still paints the focused border and inset ring with `--p`. The theme picker exposes 32 additional themes (`libs/frontend/core/src/lib/services/theme.service.ts:20-54`). Recomputing their tokens from `node_modules/daisyui/dist/themes.css` after sRGB clipping finds, for example, `cupcake` primary at **1.93:1 against base-100 and 1.72:1 against an unfocused base-200 card**, `bumblebee` at **1.38/1.13**, and `garden` at **3.12/2.55**. Thirteen of the 32 miss 3:1 against at least one required adjacent surface. **Fix:** give those themes a measured frame token with at least 3:1 against both surfaces (base-content is a viable starting fallback), and add a contrast check over all shipped theme tokens. This gap predates C2, but remains within the explicit cross-theme acceptance check.

2. **Serious — the universal 70% state mix does not make pills readable in other shipped themes.** `libs/frontend/chat-ui/src/lib/molecules/compact-session/compact-rail-card.component.ts:190-196,229-230`. The fallback state inks for built-in themes are their DaisyUI `--in`, `--wa`, `--er`, `--su` and `--bc` tokens. Recomputing the same OKLab mix and the unchanged 14% primary focused tint gives `light` running text **2.84:1 focused / 3.54:1 unfocused**, `cupcake` needs text **2.42/2.61**, and `aqua` running text **1.76/2.27**. Twenty-three of 32 built-in themes have at least one pill state below 4.5:1 on a card surface. **Fix:** choose measured per-theme text inks or an adaptive text tier for every state, and check all five states on both card surfaces in all shipped themes. Preserve the separate raw state ink for the stripe and dot. The new mix improves the two Anubis themes but cannot serve as the cross-theme guarantee.
