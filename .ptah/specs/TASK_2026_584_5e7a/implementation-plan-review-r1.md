## Notes

- **Parent End Decision Alignment (D7)**: Revision 1 completely aligns with the user's decision that children outlive the parent. The removal of the 30s grace stop for parent inactivity avoids collateral termination of child sessions. The held completion mechanism (`takeHeldCompletions`, bounded to 1 latest settle per child) and immediate rejection of child reports (`parent-session-not-active`) when the parent is inactive prevent unbounded memory growth and avoid stale replay.
- **Resumed Parent Ownership**: Reopening a parent session from the sidebar (minting a new tab ID) is accounted for by resolving ownership against `parentSdkSessionId`, allowing continued control and steering of existing child sessions.
- **Architecture & Boundaries**: The plan preserves hexagonal port conventions, respects the dependency lattice in `eslint.config.mjs`, and maintains disjoint batch boundaries for parallel development.
