## Findings

### Serious

#### 1. The running live-phase dot is nearly invisible in the light theme (WCAG 1.4.11)

- File: `libs/frontend/chat/src/lib/components/atoms/session-organization-chips/session-organization-chips.component.ts:142-146` (`w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-primary animate-pulse`).
- Viewports: all, light theme only (the dark theme measures 3.35:1, a pass).

- Fix: give the dot a ring (`border border-base-content/70`, as C2.2 round 1 did for task-card dots) or a darker fill, and re-measure at 3:1.

### Moderate

2. Sort and group selects clip the current value at 224px. The default state reads "Last a" and "No gr" (`after-org-anubis-light-1400-focus-3.png`; "By sta" and "By pa" for the other group values; "Last", "No ç" in the no-match shot `after-org-anubis-800-no-match.png`). Fix: put the selects on their own row, or shorten the option labels, or widen their share beside the Filters button.

"Investigate memo…", "Spike: openc…", "Refactor ses…" at 224px (`after-org-anubis-800-default.png`). The `title` tooltip carries the full name. Fix: move the action cluster onto the chip line, or reserve the width only for the row that is hovered or focused.
4. Nested rows wrap their metadata. In `group by parent`, level 1+ rows narrow the metadata line to two lines ("6h / ago", "20 / msgs") (`after-org-anubis-light-800-group-parent.png`, Child A of root). The depth cap works (depths 1, 2, 3, 3 at 12/24/36/36px margins, `results-after-org.json`, `group-parent`), no horizontal overflow. Fix: make the metadata `flex-wrap`-free (`whitespace-nowrap`) or reduce the title reserve at depth.
5. Faint focus rings on the filter bar controls. The search input shows a 2px ring with a transparent outline (`rgba(0,0,0,0)`), and the selects a 2px ring at 20% alpha (`oklch(... / 0.2)`). Buttons (Filters, Date range, row actions) have the 2px primary-colour ring (`oklch(0.7665 0.1387 91.06)` dark, `oklch(0.58 0.132 75)` light). Fix: use the same `focus-visible:outline` as the buttons for the input label and the selects.
