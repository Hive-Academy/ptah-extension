C. Focus-trap contract for the dialog (implementation note)

The existing trap queries button:not(:disabled) and wraps from the last to the first. All new controls are buttons: Change (aria-expanded), the two target buttons (role=radio, roving tabindex), Keep all, Replace all, each Keep/Replace radio, What changes. Controls inside the collapsed Change panel are hidden and must be excluded by the trap query (add :not([hidden] *) or test visibility). A forced-keep Replace radio uses aria-disabled (not disabled) and tabindex=-1, so it is announced but never reachable by Tab or arrow selection.
