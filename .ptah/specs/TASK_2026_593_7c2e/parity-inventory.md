## Approved Removals

Successful and neutral finished-turn completion notices and their completion-only Read all/dismiss controls are approved for removal in `context.md`. The canvas dock, needs-you chip/popover, its popover-only close behaviors, and back-office activity ticker are approved for removal in `decisions.md` #13 and implemented by TASK_2026_702_594d. The bell inherits all pending permission capabilities, including CLI-agent lane Allow/Deny/Open and the double-answer guard. Routine back-office progress has no user-facing replacement; back-office failures receive snackbar and bell failure notices. Failed-turn, ActionBanner, and voice-switch capabilities remain.
