## Decisions

1. **Use untrusted for every level**, keeping plan read-only and auto-edit/YOLO workspace-write. Alternatives: on-request or never/full access. Evidence: the approved plan separates application auto-answering from native sandbox policy; research-report.md records untrusted and the sandbox modes. Reversible in policy() after protocol evidence.
2. **One marker action per case** instead of a multi-command sandbox demonstration or two MCP calls. Evidence: this fix request requires at most one approval round and immediate interruption at the expected outcome. The removed positive-read/multi-path observations remain explicitly unverified. Reversible by an approved future budget/case revision.
3. **12,000 observed cumulative tokens, 60 seconds per case, 15 seconds per RPC**, with 1,024 output tokens retained. Alternatives: the former unbounded observed usage and 120-second wait, or a provider-side budget unsupported by the current evidence. These bounds are local and reversible; they do not claim a dollar ceiling.
4. **Retain auth on confirmed kill failure**, reporting cleanup failure, instead of deleting it while a known child may retain a live session. Evidence: the request requires kill-before-delete and no live child after deletion. This is an explicit residual limitation of finding 2, not a claim of all-death cleanup. A stronger process-container mechanism would need a separately reviewed implementation.
5. **Use an explicitly unverified decline fallback** only where the generated per-method enum is unavailable. Evidence: the current fix request permits a fallback; the local research does not establish the complete response schema. Actual method compatibility must be assessed before treating any observation as protocol proof.
