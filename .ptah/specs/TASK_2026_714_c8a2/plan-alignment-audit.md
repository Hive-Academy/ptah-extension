## Gaps and risks

2. **Blocking — live controls bypass the router, and YOLO is rejected after ordinary UI translation.** **Verified:** config handler injects concrete SDK adapter (`libs/backend/rpc-handlers/src/lib/handlers/config-rpc.handlers.ts:81`), applies permissions at :372 and effort at :706, maps `yolo` to `bypassPermissions` at :749, and catches sync failure while leaving config saved (:381/:711).
