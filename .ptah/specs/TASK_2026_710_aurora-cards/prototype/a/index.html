Ptah Aurora + Glass

Design rationale. A slow four-blob aurora (lapis, teal, gold, violet-tinted) drifts behind frosted-glass cards with a luminous gold-to-lapis hairline edge, inner top highlight and faint grain, giving depth without noise. Egyptian identity comes from gold on near-black, a Cinzel wordmark and the Ptah emblem; Lucide-style 1.5px SVG line icons replace the broken glyphs. Every colour, blur and glow is a CSS variable in tokens.css, with anubis (dark) and anubis-light variants, AA text on glass, visible focus rings, and aurora motion that stops under prefers-reduced-motion.
