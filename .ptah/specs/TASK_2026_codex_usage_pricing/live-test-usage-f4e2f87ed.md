## Defects and likely locations

1. **Requested primary/secondary limits are not both visible.** The zero-turn landing view exposes one Weekly figure, and the detailed session panel exposes only a Weekly window after account resolution. This run cannot determine whether the native account response lacked the other window or whether normalization/display dropped it. Inspect `libs/backend/cli-agent-runtime/src/lib/cli-agents/limits/plan-limit-owner-discovery.service.ts`, `libs/backend/rpc-handlers/src/lib/services/plan-limits-snapshot.service.ts`, and the frontend Plan Limits store/panel. Evidence: `E\shots\plan-zero-turn.png`, `E\shots\turn1-expanded.png`, `E\shots\plan-window-open.png`.
2. **Account resolution is briefly unavailable after restart.** The saved session's Plan Limits tile displayed **Account not resolved** shortly after restart and recovered after about 10 seconds, while token, cost, context, and model data were already restored. The same owner-discovery and snapshot paths are the likely latency points. Evidence: `E\app-restart-expanded.json`, `E\shots\plan-after-restart-wait.png`, `E\log-findings.json`.
