## Proposed Removals

User decisions #9 and #10 approve removing the entire canvas dock row, including its back-office ticker and canvas-only Thoth click target. Thoth remains reachable from its own page. Chip unmount and deletion of the four queue implementation/spec files happen only in this task's last phase, after TASK_2026_593_7c2e merges its replacement bell with attention and lane-permission coverage. Other dock-removal work does not wait for that merge. The New Session flow and all three warning texts move to the locations above; warning notices are inline, not snackbars. No analytics event was found in the inspected components; preserve any host instrumentation discovered during implementation rather than inventing an event.
