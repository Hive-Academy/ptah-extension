## Prototype size follow-up

The 816 KB HTML was dominated by one embedded 512×512 Ptah logo PNG: 575,183 decoded bytes and 766,912 Base64 characters. No fonts or libraries were embedded. I replaced that image data with a 128×128 WebP copy of the same logo (8,178 bytes), sufficient for its maximum 64px display size. The standalone HTML is now 47,654 bytes; its remaining inline CSS, JavaScript, and small SVG icons support the interactive fixture. The single-chat screenshots were recaptured after the change.
