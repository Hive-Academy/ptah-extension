## Findings

1. **[Serious] Handed-off canvas rail card uses the full 176px live-card height with an empty 70px lane block and 22px footer (`design-spec.md:122`, `prototype/index.html:126, 164, 458-465`, `prototype/screenshots/dark-populated-wide.png`, `light-populated-wide.png`, `dark-long-chain.png`, `dark-populated-narrow.png`)**
   - **Location**: `design-spec.md:122` (`Canvas rail card (Handed off)`), `prototype/index.html:126, 164, 458-465`, and target components `libs/frontend/chat-ui/src/lib/molecules/compact-session/compact-rail-card.component.ts:47-48, 99-105, 256` and `libs/frontend/canvas/src/lib/canvas-rail/canvas-rail-card.component.ts:81-82`.
   - **Problem**: `design-spec.md:122` states: *"Card height stays 176px comfortable or 96px dense (`COMPACT_RAIL_CARD_HEIGHT`, `compact-lane.types.ts` lines 87–92)"* and *"The lane block is empty: no lane row, no running count, no pulse."* In `compact-rail-card.component.ts:99-102`, the `176px` comfortable height exists because `.rc-body` reserves a `70px` row for `.rc-lanes` (`grid-template-rows: 26px 16px 70px 22px`) plus a `22px` footer row. Because a handed-off session is stopped and all live lanes/children were adopted by the successor, keeping `176px` leaves **~110px of blank empty space** in every handed-off card (clearly visible in `dark-populated-wide.png`, `light-populated-wide.png`, and `dark-long-chain.png`). Visually, the dead predecessor card has the same vertical weight as the live `Running` successor card above it, and in an `A → B → C` chain two handed-off cards consume `352px` of vertical rail space, pushing other active sessions off-screen.
   - **What to change**: Specify a **compact handed-off card height** in `design-spec.md:122, 171-172` and `prototype/index.html:126, 151-153, 164`:
     - When `handedOff()` is `true` on `ptah-compact-rail-card` (`compact-rail-card.component.ts:99-105, 217-223`), omit/collapse the empty `.rc-lanes` (`70px`) and `.rc-foot` (`22px`) rows so `.rc-body` only renders the `26px` title + `Handed off` pill row and the `16px` meta row (`branch · duration`) with `10px` top/bottom padding (`~60px–64px` total height in `comfortable` density, `~44px–48px` in `dense` density).
     - Update `[style.height.px]` on `li[ptah-canvas-rail-card]` (`canvas-rail-card.component.ts:81-82`) to use that compact handed-off height (e.g., `COMPACT_RAIL_HANDED_OFF_HEIGHT = { comfortable: 62, dense: 46 }` or equivalent token in `compact-lane.types.ts`) when `!collapsed() && handedOff()`.
     - Update `.rail-card.handed-off` in `prototype/index.html` and recapture the screenshots so the handed-off card renders as a compact subordinate card beneath the `176px` live successor card.

   - **Location**: `design-spec.md:134`, `prototype/index.html:151-153, 401, 483-500`, `prototype/README.md:25`.
   - **Problem**: `task-description.md` Criterion 6 (`lines 354-356`) requires: *"For A → B → C with all three cards open, B sits under C and A sits under B, in C's group, and only C is counted in that group."* While the second and third sentences of `design-spec.md:134` describe multi-level indent (`12px`, `24px`, `36px` matching `ml-3` / `ml-6` / `ml-9`), the first sentence (*"Only the immediate predecessor nests under its successor"*) was implemented in `prototype/index.html:401` (`"Only the immediate predecessor nests on the rail"`) and `renderRail()` (`prototype/index.html:483-500`) by rendering **only two cards** (`chain[newest]` and `chain[newest - 1]`) and dropping `A` ("Plan the inspector") in `populated` (`A → B → C`) and `A..D` in `long`.    - **What to change**: Reword `design-spec.md:134` to state unambiguously that every open handed-off card in the chain whose immediate successor is also open on the rail renders in the chain's rail group indented one step deeper than its immediate successor (`B` at `12px` / `ml-3`, `A` at `24px` / `ml-6`, deeper ancestors capped at `36px` / `ml-9`), and update `renderRail()` in `prototype/index.html:483-500` so `populated` (`A → B → C`) and `long` render all open chain cards with `.nest-1`, `.nest-2`, `.nest-3`.

4. **[Moderate] Component mapping omits guarding rail indentation when the canvas rail is collapsed (`w-[56px]`) and gating `<span class="sr-only">Child session:</span>` when `[depth]` is reused on `ptah-session-list-row` (`design-spec.md:134, 159, 172-174`)**
   - **Location**: `design-spec.md:134, 159, 172-174`, `libs/frontend/canvas/src/lib/canvas-rail/canvas-rail.component.ts:108, 265-266`, `libs/frontend/canvas/src/lib/canvas-rail/canvas-rail-card.component.ts:81-88`, and `libs/frontend/chat/src/lib/components/molecules/session-list-row/session-list-row.component.ts:219-221`.
   - **Problem**:
     - **Collapsed 56px canvas rail**: When `ptah-canvas-rail` is collapsed (`collapsed() === true`), the rail width is `56px` (`canvas-rail.component.ts:108`) and each `li[ptah-canvas-rail-card]` renders a compact `56px` tile (`canvas-rail-card.component.ts:81-82, 120`). If `ml-3` (`12px`), `ml-6` (`24px`), or `ml-9` (`36px`) is applied while `collapsed()` is `true`, nested cards are pushed out of the `56px` rail and clipped.
     - **Screen-reader `"Child session:"` prefix**: `design-spec.md:159` states *"Previous-sessions: do not add 'Child session' screen-reader text for this indent (that string is for parent grouping, `session-list-row.component.ts` lines 219–221)"*, while `design-spec.md:173-174` reuses the existing `[depth]="row.depth"` input on `ptah-session-list-row`. In `session-list-row.component.ts:219-221`, `<span class="sr-only">Child session:</span>` is rendered unconditionally whenever `depth() > 0`. Reusing `[depth]` without modifying line `219` will cause screen readers to announce `"Child session:"` for handed-off predecessors.
   - **What to change**:
     - In `design-spec.md:134, 172`, specify that `ml-3` / `ml-6` / `ml-9` on `li[ptah-canvas-rail-card]` applies only when `!collapsed()`.
     - In `design-spec.md:159, 173`, explicitly specify gating `<span class="sr-only">Child session:</span>` in `session-list-row.component.ts:219-221` (e.g., `@if (depth() > 0 && !handedOff())`) so handoff-nested rows do not announce `"Child session:"`.
