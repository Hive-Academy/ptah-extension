# Ptah Content Brand

Content brand for Ptah copy (landing, docs, release notes). Visual tokens live in `DESIGN.md` at the repository root and in `apps/ptah-landing-page/tailwind.config.js`.

## Positioning

Source: `apps/ptah-landing-page/src/index.html`.

- Name: PtahCode.
- Title: "PtahCode — Your AI dev team. Free and open source."
- Description: a free, MIT-licensed desktop app that runs a team of AI agents on your own machine. Bring any model, open-weight or frontier, and keep your code and keys with you.

## Rules

- The product is the Electron desktop app. Do not describe a VS Code extension, a headless CLI product or a hosted service as current.
- Every capability claim maps to code in this repository. No invented numbers, testimonials or logos.
- Visual direction: dark ink background with one amber accent, Inter and JetBrains Mono. See `DESIGN.md`.
